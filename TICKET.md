## Grading Rubric

> **For instructors and /teach-check assistant** — Students may skip this section.

**Assessment method:** Document submission + understanding verification through Q&A.

**Reading verification (required):**

1. **Ask:** "What's one thing you learned from the dev-guide docs?"
   - Good answer: Names specific details (file locations, commands, architecture components)
   - Bad answer: "I don't remember" or vague generalities

2. **Ask:** "What's one thing you learned from the decision docs?"
   - Good answer: Can explain a specific decision and its reasoning
   - Bad answer: Can't recall any decisions

**Core understanding (required):**

3. **Ask:** "What's the difference between decisions and dev-guide docs?"
   - Good answer: "Decisions explain WHY we made choices. Dev-guide explains HOW to work with the code."
   - Acceptable: "Decisions = reasons, Dev-guide = instructions"
   - Bad answer: Can't distinguish between them

4. **Ask:** "When should you write documentation?"
   - Good answer: "At milestones, while memory is fresh" or "As you go, not at the end"
   - Bad answer: "At the end of the project" or "When someone asks for it"

**Document submission (required):**

5. **Check submitted document:**
   - Located in `docs/` directory
   - Has clear title
   - Has recognizable structure (Background/Solution/Key Points or similar)
   - Covers one coherent topic
   - Would be useful to a future reader

6. **Ask:** "Why did you choose this topic?"
   - Good answer: Can explain personal relevance (bug they hit, concept that confused them, etc.)
   - Bad answer: "I just picked something random"

**What counts as "pass":**

- Read all 8 documents (4 dev-guide + 4 decisions)
- Can distinguish between decisions and dev-guide
- Submitted a document with proper structure
- Can explain topic choice and what they learned

**What does NOT count as pass:**

- Skipped reading the documents
- Cannot explain the difference between doc types
- No document submitted
- Document has no structure or is copy-pasted from elsewhere
- Cannot explain why documentation matters
